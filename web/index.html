For friends planning a night out

Common Ground

Everyone names a few bands, films, shows or books they love. Qloo's taste graph turns that shared taste into dinner, drinks and live music in your city. Each stop shows whose taste picked it.

Checking the data source.

Plan the night

Add 2 to 6 friends. Give each friend 1 to 4 favourites, separated by commas. Mix music, films, TV and books.

Add a date and Qloo keeps places open that weekday. Exact hours, travel and availability are still not checked.

Or pick one thing together

One movie, show, book, artist or restaurant for the group, picked the same fair way.

Use it from your agent

Both flows are MCP tools, plan_night_out and find_common_ground. Add them to Claude Code with one command.

claude mcp add --transport http common-ground https://common-ground.lipmichal.workers.dev/mcp

Then ask your agent. "Plan a night out in Lisbon. Ana loves Radiohead and Dune, Tom loves Kendrick Lamar and The Bear, Priya loves Phoebe Bridgers and Normal People."
